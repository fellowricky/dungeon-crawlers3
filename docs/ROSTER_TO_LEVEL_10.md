# Roster to level 10 — review and ideas

Written 2026-11-01 while Zenu was away. Covers: a review of every dungeon floor
built so far (L1–L6), ideas to take all five themes to L10, and what was queued
for generation that night. **Nothing here is wired into the game.**

Counting rule (unchanged): a level is done with ≥ 4 theme-exclusive animated mooks
at its anchor CR plus an animated boss. L7 = CR 4, L8 = CR 5, L9 = CR 6, L10 = CR 7.

---

## 1. Review of L1–L6

| Theme | State | What works | What to watch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ancient** | L1–L6 wired | The cleanest ladder in the game: one goblinoid escalation (kobold → goblin → hobgoblin → bugbear → gnoll → orc), each a distinct silhouette and armour budget. | The ladder is **spent** at L6. Another "bigger humanoid tribe" at L7 would be a seventh repeat; L7+ needs a new owner. |
| **Verdant** | L1–L6 wired | L5 and L6 deliberately break scale (all rider / all body), which rescued four tiers of small forest folk that had started to blur. | `treant` is still the L7 boss but Old Growth (L6) already *is* the trees. Move the tree boss down or retire it. |
| **Grim** | L1–L5 wired, L6 built | The strongest *ideas*: tithe-takers, the ward, the Unrelieved's "nothing matches" rule, the Cerement's inversion of it. | Five tiers of human-sized undead or cultists. L7+ must change body plan (beasts, bats, incorporeal) or it will start to blur the way verdant did. |
| **Frost** | L1–L2 wired, L3–L6 built | The giant's-hold spine (drudges → constructs → huscarls → court) finally gives frost a structure, and it uses snowfield's unused Giantkind weighting. | By L6 the hold is climbed: L7 can't be "more giants". All four built tiers are blue-grey iron — the theme has **one palette**. |
| **Molten** | L1 wired, L2–L6 built | The most coherent theme on paper: one infernal legion seen at five ranks, with a strict dark-body / heat-as-accent palette rule. | Same risk as frost from the other side: five tiers of one legion in one palette. L7+ should **leave the legion**, not promote it again. |

**The frost/molten problem in one line:** both themes were built as *one culture
climbing one ladder in one palette*, so each new tier has less room to look new.
The fix is not more ice and more fire — it is new **owners** of the cold and the
heat: people and things that live there for reasons of their own.

## 2. Ideas to L10, per theme

Bold = queued tonight. Others are ideas, roughly ranked by confidence.

### Grim (CR 4 → 7)
- **L7 — the Moonbound (werewolves).** *Queued.* A cursed noble hunting lodge whose household turned. Beasts at last: digitigrade, four-legged and huge silhouettes after five tiers of men. Packrunner, Tracker (human huntsman, bitten), Moon-Crone, Ironmuzzle (chained brute), Moonhound, boss the **Packfather** (a fallen lord, crown askew).
- **L8 — House Morrow (vampires).** *Queued.* One livery (black, crimson bat-wing crest), which is the Cerement's "matched" rule in a new key. Thrall Footman, Arbalist, Blood Chaplain, Coffin-Bearer, Vampire Spawn, boss the **Vampire Knight** (current boss `vampire-knight`).
- L9 — **the Shadow Court** (moved from L7): wraiths, spectres, a poltergeist elite. The first incorporeal tier — the engine can express it as `invisible`/phasing.
- L10 — **the Wight-King's barrow**: one king, one era, his whole army buried with him (the Unrelieved's rule inverted a second time).
- Alt: a **witch-hunter order gone rotten** (living humans hunting the dead, silver and pitch) as a palette break at L9.

### Ancient (CR 4 → 7)
- **L7 — the Deepquarry.** *Queued.* Stone giants who quarry the halls with ogre labour gangs. Ogre Hauler, young stone giant Hurler, Dreamwalker (stone giants' dream-shamans), Slabback ogre, Chainmaster ogre, boss **Stone Giant Lord** (already `stone-giant-lord`).
- L8 — the **Petrified Court** of the medusa: gorgon-touched guards, basilisk hounds, "statues" that move.
- L9 — **drow raiders** with the behir as their war-beast.
- L10 — the **aboleth's thralls**: earlier races (kobold, hobgoblin, orc) reused with a new tell — mucus sheen, slack faces, a third eye. Cheap to make look new because the silhouettes already exist.

### Verdant (CR 4 → 7)
- L7 — **the Drowned**: a flooded underwood of merrow, sea-hag kin of the Grandmother, drowned knights.
- **L8 — the Hydra-Sworn (lizardfolk).** *Queued.* Clans who worship the hydra: Clubber (obsidian club), Archer, Hydra Shaman, Shellback (turtle-shell shield), War Crocodile, boss the **Hydra** (five heads).
- L9 — the **chimera's crag**: perytons, harpies, a manticore elite.
- L10 — the **roc's eyrie**: griffon riders and giant eagles.

### Frost (CR 4 → 7) — away from ice
- **L7 — the Hungering.** *Queued.* A starving winter cannibal cult around a wendigo. Horror rather than ice: Flenser, Bone-thrower, Antler-Priest, the Gorged (bloated, in a bear hide), the Turning (half-wendigo), boss **the Wendigo**. Bone, grey fur and frost-bitten skin — the theme's first non-iron palette.
- L8 — the **Remorhaz warrens**: thermal tunnels under the ice, fire-bellied beasts. Heat in a cold biome is the palette break frost needs.
- L9 — **the sunken longship / the drowned crew**: an ice-locked raider ship and its frozen dead (draugr), *if* Grim's L10 does not take the same idea.
- L10 — the **white dragon and its kobold-turned-cultists**, or a **yeti / abominable** clan with mammoth riders.
- Alt: **the Hold's prisoners** — what the frost giants kept in their pits (an ettin, ogre slaves, a chained beholderkin) — reuses the L6 setting with new creatures.

### Molten (CR 4 → 7) — away from the legion
- **L7 — the Graythane Delve (duergar).** *Queued.* Grey dwarves mining the volcano for their own ends: Hammerer, Bolter, Mind-Binder (psionic, violet), Stone Guard, the Enlarged (swollen to giant size by their own magic), boss the **Forge-Thane**. Iron and soot like the legion, but ash-grey skin and a **violet** psionic accent instead of fire.
- L8 — the **fire giants' forge**: the legion's smiths; the first *huge* molten silhouette.
- L9 — **the vrock's pit**: demons, not devils — green-black, feathers and filth. A deliberate palette break.
- L10 — **the efreeti's brass city**, or a **red dragon cult** (salamander priests, kobold dragonscales).
- Alt: **the azer** (bronze-skinned fire dwarves) — skip: too close to the duergar.
