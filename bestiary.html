⚖️ CR Monster Stat Scaling

Every monster's statblock is multiplied by the row matching its Challenge Rating: scaled = Math.floor(base × (1 + pct / 100)). A CR that isn't listed uses the nearest lower row. Raise HP % and Atk % to make fights longer and deadlier, raise AC % to make monsters harder to hit, and Stat % to lift ability scores, saves and DCs. Edits apply live to the Bestiary statblocks and the Arena preview; hit Save to Codebase to write them into src/game/cr_scaling.json for the game.

Curve tool. Instead of typing 24 rows by hand, sweep one column from a start value at the low CR to an end value at the high CR and let a shape decide what happens in between. Ease in keeps early CRs gentle then ramps hard (good when low-level fights feel punishing); ease out front-loads the difficulty and flattens at the top; S-curve keeps both ends calm and does the work in the middle band. The sweep is spread evenly across the rows in range, not the raw CR numbers, so the fractional CRs at the bottom of the table each get their own step.

🐾 Room Spawn Budget

Every combat/elite/boss room gets a threat budget and then buys monsters with it: budget = baseBudget × questThreatMult × levelCurve[dungeonLevel] × (floor + span × roomDifficulty) × typeMult[roomType]. The generator buys monsters at the price of the CR that dungeon level actually fields (crAnchorLevels: CR 0.25 at level 1, CR 10 at 16+) until the budget runs dry or the spawn cap is hit. Cost follows a continuous curve crToCost(cr), scaled by a single crCostScale multiplier — so raising the scale thins out every room at every depth, and raising the base budget packs rooms fuller. Room difficulty runs 0 near the entrance to 1 at the deepest room. Hit Save to Codebase to write src/game/spawn_budget.json, then reload the game.

📈 CR Anchor Points Controls which CR ratings are possible at each dungeon depth — anchors define the interpolation curve that selects monster tiers

Each anchor pairs a dungeon level with a target Challenge Rating. CR at levels between anchors is interpolated linearly; there is also a roll-up chance proportional to how far the level sits between two anchors. The first anchor binds the minimum, the last binds the maximum. Two or more anchors are required.

📉 Per-Dungeon-Level Curve The depth axis: how much of the room budget a dungeon of this level actually gets

Room difficulty only ranks rooms within one floor, so without this curve a level 1 dungeon's back rooms are packed exactly as hard as a level 20 one's. Budget × scales that floor's whole spawn budget; Spawn cap is the hard ceiling on monsters per room — it also trims themed squads (swarms, nests, miniboss escorts) and overland clearings, which otherwise ignore the budget entirely.

📜 Story Quest Curation

Each chapter pins its own cast: a mob pool per floor, a fallback roster for any floor with no pool of its own, and the curated finale — boss creature, display name, HP multiplier and escort minions. A whitelisted floor spawns only from its list: no CR window, no level ceiling, no falling back to the rest of the bestiary. What varies is how much of the cast turns up, governed by that floor's own CR budget. Edits are saved as overrides keyed by mission id, so the authored prose in story.js is never rewritten.
