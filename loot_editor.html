🛡️ Chassis
Base items carry no stat bonuses. A base is only its physical chassis — damage dice, body-armour AC, and a shield's or helm's own protection. Every stat (STR, HP, crit, saves, elemental damage…) comes from affixes, which is what the point budget actually buys. Set the damage dice and armour class in the 5e Properties section below.
🎲 Allowed Affixes
Restrict which affixes can roll on this base. Leave everything ticked (the default) and the base rolls the whole pool — the whitelist only ever subtracts. Affixes are still filtered by their own slot and item-level rules on top of this.
