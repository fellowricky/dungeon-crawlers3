#LANDSCAPE
A low, ancient drystone sheep-fold on a bleak grey moor, and its encircling wall has burst outward — grey stones flung and scattered in a fan across the surrounding grass, the wall shoved apart from the inside rather than fallen in. Within the broken ring of the fold, the turf has slumped into a shallow muddy bowl, and through the mud at its centre shows a course of fitted, jointed, dressed grey stone — precise ancient masonry surfacing from below, utterly unlike the rough drystone wall around it.

The moor runs away flat and empty under a low overcast sky, coarse grass and heather and standing pools to a misty horizon. No people, no animals, no fire, no smoke. Cold muted palette of wet grey stone, drab green turf, peat brown mud and dead pale sky. Mood of something buried and deliberate pushing up through a shepherd's humble wall.
