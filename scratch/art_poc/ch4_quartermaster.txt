#WARM
A warm, bustling scene of a mercenary-charter quartermaster's kit-wagon parked in a torchlit muster-yard between bouts, richly lit. Only distant, out-of-focus background figures at the edges — no clear faces in the foreground. There is no readable writing of any kind. The centre is a big four-wheeled supply wagon, its canvas sides rolled up to show a stocked interior: neat racks of gear, coils of good rope, stacked crates, hanging lanterns, water-skins, folded bedrolls, tools of the trade — a treasure-trove of well-kept equipment gleaming in warm lamplight. A hand-painted odds-shingle and a pennant hang from the wagon's frame (blank, no legible text). One wheel is chocked and a suspicious extra chain runs to a locked strongbox under the axle — the catch in the game. Warm firelight and hanging lamps pour golden light over polished wood, brass fittings and canvas, banners strung overhead, the packed muster-yard glowing behind. Palette of warm gold, amber lamplight, honey-brown wood and rich banner colours. Strictly pre-industrial medieval fantasy, nothing modern anywhere. Mood of a lively, well-run camp market with a sly catch in it.
