An underground chamber of flawless ancient dressed-stone masonry — huge precisely fitted grey ashlar blocks in tight courses, far older and finer than anything on the surface. At the head of a dark descending stair, set squarely into the wall and facing outward toward the viewer, is a great marker-stone deep-carved with a stark abstract geometric warning sigil: hard angular lines and a bold barred threshold-mark, machine-exact, cut by people who expected to be obeyed. It is a purely abstract carved symbol, no letters, no runes, no writing.

The marker plainly faces up the stairs, positioned to be read by anyone descending from above and to turn them back. A faint cold blue-grey light rakes across the carved grooves and the fitted stone; deep black shadow fills the stair going down behind it. Drifting dust. Cold palette of grey dressed stone, black shadow, and a faint blue-white glow in the carved mark. Mood of an ancient, authoritative KEEP OUT set at a threshold the party is about to cross anyway.
