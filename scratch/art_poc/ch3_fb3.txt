An ancient descending stone stairway going down into darkness, lit cold and dim. Laid carefully along the steps, in descending order, is a garrison's abandoned kit: near the top, swords, spears and bows set neatly against the wall; further down, stacked mail hauberks, helms and greaves, unbuckled and piled with care; lower still, rows of boots, set side by side. Nothing is dropped or scattered — everything is placed deliberately, squared to the wall, the way pilgrims lay aside their belongings at a threshold. The soldiers shed their arms and armour step by step as they descended.

The stair is dressed grey stone, older than any kingdom, vanishing into black below. A faint cold blue-grey light from deep down catches the laid-out gear. No people, no bodies here — only what they set down. Cold palette of grey stone, dull steel, worn leather, deep shadow. Mood of a calm, terrible divestment before something waiting below.
