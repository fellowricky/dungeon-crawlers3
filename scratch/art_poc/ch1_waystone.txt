A close, intimate scene of a single ancient standing stone in a small forest clearing, at overcast twilight. No people, no creatures and no characters appear anywhere in the scene.

The waystone dominates the composition slightly left of centre: a tall weathered granite menhir about twice the height of a man, leaning very slightly, its surface pitted and furred with grey-green lichen and old moss. Its faces are carved with worn abstract ornament — deep-cut interlocking spirals, concentric rings, knotwork bands and simple geometric chevrons. These carvings are purely abstract decorative patterns, not letters, not runes, not words and not writing of any kind.

The stone has been crudely defaced by goblins. Slashes and handprints of thick ochre, sickly yellow and dried-blood red war-paint are daubed violently across its lower two thirds, dripping and half-flaked, along with a few crude smeared claw-mark tallies. The paint is ugly, recent and obviously alien to the stone's careful old craftsmanship. A pair of small broken goblin fetishes — bundled sticks, feathers and knucklebones on frayed cord — hang from an iron peg driven into the rock.

Beneath and between the war-paint, the stone's original blessing still lives: the deepest carved grooves glow with a soft, cool blue-white inner light, faint but steady, bleeding gently through and around the smeared paint that tries to cover it. This soft glow is the only cool light in the scene and it pools faintly on the wet grass and stones at the menhir's base.

The clearing around it is a ring of damp trampled grass, mushrooms, fallen leaves and a few flat half-sunken kerb stones marking an old ritual circle, ringed tightly by tall dark pine trunks that close the composition in. Low mist creeps between the trunks. The canopy above is heavy and nearly closed, leaving only a dim bruised grey-purple sliver of sky.

Mood of desecrated sanctity and stubborn quiet power. Muted palette of wet greys, mossy greens, damp browns and the harsh ochre-red of the paint, against the single cool blue glow.
