#WARM
A warm, lamplit outfitter's table inside a grand guildhall, piled with fine adventuring gear — a mail shirt, a good sword, boots, a cloak, all neat and new and each stamped with a small abstract geometric guild sigil. Beside the gear, open on the table, is a great leather-bound ledger with columns of entries in illegible clerkly handwriting (rendered as fine wavering ink strokes, no readable letters or numbers), a quill in an inkwell, and a small stack of coins. The scene reads as generosity with a hidden price — gifts being carefully tallied as debt.

Warm brass lamplight pools on the polished wood, the gear and the ledger page. Rich honey-brown tones, gleam of steel, cream parchment. No people, only their hands' work left on the table. Mood of a warm open hand that is quietly writing you into its books.
