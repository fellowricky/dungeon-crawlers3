#WARM
A solemn, warmly-lit interior scene of the great memorial wall of a mercenary charter-hall — a towering slab of dark grey slate honouring dead companies, lit by ranked votive candles. No people appear anywhere in the scene. CRITICAL: the slate wall is smooth, plain and blank — it carries NO writing, NO text, NO letters, NO runes, NO inscriptions and NO rows of glyphs of any kind. Its only ornament is a sparse, widely-spaced grid of small carved heraldic emblems — little shields, blazons and company crests cut into the stone — with wide areas of bare smooth slate between them, and a few short tally-notch clusters. Absolutely no letterforms or writing anywhere. The vast dark slate wall fills the frame, its sparse emblem-rows receding upward into shadow, worn smooth and dark at the bottom and fresher toward the top. A low stone shelf at its base holds many small guttering candles and pooled wax, their warm light washing gold up the lower stone and dying into deep blue-black shadow above. A few faded plain pennants of vanished companies hang alongside. Palette of warm candle-gold and amber against cold dark slate-grey and deep shadow. Strictly pre-industrial medieval fantasy. Mood of overwhelming, quiet weight — the honoured dead beyond counting.
