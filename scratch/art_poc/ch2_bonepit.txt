A close, oppressive scene deep inside a narrow underground bone-pit passage cut through dressed stone, dim and cold. No people and no creatures appear in the scene. The rough-hewn stone throat of the passage narrows toward the viewer, its dressed walls scored chest-high with long parallel horn-gouges and hoof-scrapes where a stampede of cattle was driven through in the dark. The floor is a churned drift of splintered white bones, cracked skulls and trampled horn, packed and crusted. A faint sourceless grey light filters from somewhere above and ahead, catching the pale bone and the raw scored stone; the corners fall away into deep blue-black shadow. Damp stains streak the walls. Palette of bone-white and grey dressed stone against cold blue-black shadow, with sickly greenish damp. Mood of a killing-chute — the walls themselves remembering the panic that came through here.
