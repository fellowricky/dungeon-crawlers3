A dim, cold interior of a stone watchtower guardroom, lit by one weak shaft of grey daylight and a low guttering torch. Set into the middle of the flagstone floor is a descending stone stairway going down into darkness — and it is plainly far older than the tower around it, its dressed steps worn into smooth hollows by countless feet, the wear all on the descending edge of each step. The tower's rougher, newer masonry is visibly built on top of and around this ancient stair, as if raised to cover it.

The guardroom is orderly and abandoned — a bench, a folded blanket, a spear against the wall, all coated in still dust. Cobwebs in the corners. Cold palette of grey stone, deep black shadow down the stairwell, one faint warm torch-glow. Mood of a hole the tower was built to guard, its steps hollowed by journeys that only ever went down.
