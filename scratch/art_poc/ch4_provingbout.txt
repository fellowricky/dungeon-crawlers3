#WARM
A warm but tense scene of a fighting-ring arena in a mercenary-charter yard, packed gallery, torchlit as for a show. Spectators appear only as a distant, densely-massed crowd of loose silhouettes ringing the arena — no clear individual faces in the foreground. There is no readable writing of any kind. The centre is an empty roped-and-posted fighting ring of raked sand, lit hard by ringside torches and hanging braziers; across it, on the far side, a heavy iron-barred cage holds a big hunched dark shape, only half-lit, restless. Tiered wooden galleries crowded with cheering onlookers rise on all sides, strung with pennants and banners, the warm firelight catching thousands of faces reduced to a glowing blur. Drifting haze and sparks hang in the torch-light over the sand. Palette of hot torch-orange and warm gold over pale raked sand, against deep crowd-shadow and the black bulk of the caged thing. Strictly pre-industrial medieval fantasy. Mood of a paid-for spectacle about to turn real — bloodsport dressed as ceremony.
