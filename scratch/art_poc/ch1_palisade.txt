A close, looming scene of a goblin warcamp's palisade gate at night, seen from the attacker's side, torchlit. No clearly visible faces or figures — only distant hunched sentry silhouettes on the wall-walk. A tall stockade of lashed, sharpened tree-trunk stakes fills the frame, blackened and crude, its heavy timber gate barred and bristling with spikes. Guttering torches in iron brackets throw pools of hot orange light up the stakes and cast long shadows down toward the viewer; skulls, hide banners and bone fetishes are nailed above the gate as trophies. Trampled muddy ground strewn with bones and refuse runs up to the wall. Beyond the stakes the orange glow of the inner camp lights the underside of drifting smoke. Palette of hot torch-orange and ember-red against deep night-black timber and cold blue shadow. Mood of a fortified threshold — the last dark moment before an assault begins.
