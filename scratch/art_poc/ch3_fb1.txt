#LANDSCAPE
A bleak muster-ground of churned wet mud in front of a squat grey stone watchtower gate on open moorland, grey overcast light. The mud is covered in ordered files of bootprints — neat parallel rows of soldiers' tracks, evenly spaced and disciplined, all leading in one direction: inward, through the tower's open gate. Not a single set of prints returns outward. The gate stands wide open and undamaged. No people, no bodies.

The moor stretches away flat, treeless and empty behind, coarse grey-green grass to a misty horizon under heavy cloud. No fire, no smoke. Muted cold greys, wet brown mud, drab moor green. Mood of a whole garrison that formed up in good order and marched willingly into a place none of them walked out of.
