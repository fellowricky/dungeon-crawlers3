#WARM
A warm, torchlit interior of a grand guildhall at night, festive and busy. The focus is a large dark wooden roster board on the wall. The roster entries are NOT words and NOT letters — each entry is a single short horizontal chalk stroke, a plain scratched dash, stacked in neat rows like a tally sheet. There is no writing, no letters and no legible text anywhere on the board, only rows of chalk dashes. Near the top the dashes are crisp and bright; lower down, one row has been wiped into a broad grey smear to clear it.

At the right stands a single clerk, a young man in a simple sleeveless medieval tunic, seen from behind and slightly to the side. He has correct natural human anatomy: exactly two arms, both properly joined at the shoulders, hands intact. One arm is raised, elbow bent, pressing a folded rag flat against the board to wipe the smeared row — the raised arm clearly and naturally connected at his shoulder, in a relaxed believable pose. His other arm simply hangs down at his side. A stub of chalk and a spare rag rest on the board's ledge below.

Behind him the hall glows with warm lantern and firelight, wooden panelling, hanging banners in bright solid colours with simple abstract geometric shapes and no letters, and a soft blur of a few celebrating figures. Warm palette of golden lamplight, honey wood, chalk-white and grey smear. Clean correct anatomy — no extra arms, no floating or detached limbs, no misplaced arms. Mood of a cheerful hall where the dead are quietly wiped away to make room for the newly signed.
