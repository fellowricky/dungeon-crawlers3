#LANDSCAPE
A wide, still, eerie establishing shot of a lone stone watchtower standing abandoned on empty moorland under a heavy overcast sky, seen from the open ground a little below it. No people are visible anywhere in the scene, and no bodies — the place is simply, wrongly empty. There is no written lettering of any kind.

The watchtower sits slightly right of centre in the middle distance: a squat, solid, square-built military tower of dark grey fieldstone, four storeys tall, with a crenellated top, narrow arrow-slit windows and a low curtain wall of drystone enclosing a small bare courtyard. It is in good repair — no ruin, no collapse, no fire damage, no battle damage, no scorching, no broken masonry, no rubble. Nothing here has been attacked. The stonework is merely weathered and streaked with damp and lichen.

The critical detail: the tower's heavy iron-banded timber gate stands wide open, swung fully inward and undamaged, with no bar across it and nothing blocking the way. Beyond the open gate the courtyard is visible and completely empty — bare flagstones, a well, a rack of spears still standing upright, a cart, a water trough. Everything is orderly and in its place. Nothing is knocked over, nothing is scattered, nothing is broken.

From the tower's roof, a large military banner still flies from its pole, faded and weather-frayed but whole and clearly still flying, snapping stiffly in the wind. The banner is a plain solid-coloured field with a simple abstract geometric heraldic shape on it and absolutely no letters, no words and no writing.

Around the tower the moor runs out flat and treeless in every direction — coarse grey-green grass, heather, peat, standing pools, low humps of ground — to a distant featureless horizon under a ceiling of unbroken slate-grey cloud. A rutted track leads from the foreground up to the open gate. Wind bends the grass in long waves. There are no fires, no smoke, no birds circling, no movement at all.

Cold, flat, shadowless overcast daylight. Palette of wet grey stone, drab olive and brown moor, slate sky, and one muted colour accent from the banner. Mood of complete, orderly, unexplained abandonment — a fortified place left standing open on purpose, and absolute silence.
