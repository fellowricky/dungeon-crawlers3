A close, claustrophobic scene of a vault service-door hastily bricked shut from the inside using file-boxes as bricks, in a dank underground archive, dim cold light. No people and no creatures appear in the scene. There is no readable writing of any kind. The centre is a low stone doorway crudely walled up: instead of proper masonry, the gap has been stuffed and stacked with tin and wooden document-boxes, ledger-cases and bound bundles, wedged in sideways and mortared over sloppily in obvious panic and haste, some boxes crushed, edges jutting, gaps hastily daubed with mud and plaster. A few spilled, water-stained rolls and cases lie fallen at the foot of the blockage. The surrounding archive is close and wet: sagging shelves, mildewed bundles, dripping vaulted stone, black water pooling on the flagstones. A single failing lantern-glow from one side rakes the improvised wall and dies into blue-black dark. Palette of damp brown boxes and mildew against cold wet grey stone and deep shadow, one sickly lantern-amber. Mood of desperate concealment — someone bricking themselves away from whatever was coming.
