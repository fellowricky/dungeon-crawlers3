#LANDSCAPE
A bleak open moor under low grey overcast, with a wide churned cattle-track of trampled mud running through coarse grey-green grass into the distance. Strewn along the verge of the track, in a loose running line that leads the eye into the distance, is abandoned goblin war-gear: a cracked wicker-and-hide shield lying flat, a couple of crude spears dropped in the mud, a dented war-horn, a notched rusty blade, a scatter of bone fetishes and a broken helmet. The gear is thrown down carelessly, mid-flight, all trailing the same direction along the track.

There are no bodies and no goblins — only their discarded weapons lying in the mud where they were flung. The moor is empty, treeless, exposed, running to a flat misty horizon. Standing pools of brown water reflect the dull sky. No fire, no smoke. Muted cold greys, drab olive-green, peat brown and the dull iron and hide of the dropped gear. Mood of a rout without a battle — a whole warband that dropped everything and ran.
