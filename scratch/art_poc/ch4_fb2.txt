#WARM
A long dark slate memorial wall inside a warm guildhall, its polished black surface carved row upon row with the names of dead companies — rendered as neat illegible carved marks, not readable words. A stonemason's mallet and chisel rest against the wall mid-work, with a fresh half-finished name just begun and pale stone dust scattered on the floor beneath it, as if the carving of new names never stops. A small oil lamp lights the mason's spot with a warm glow.

The wall recedes into warm shadow, endless with names. Warm firelight from the hall glints off the polished slate. No people. Palette of black slate, warm gold lamplight, pale stone dust. Mood of a death-wall being carved ahead of the deaths, patient and industrial under a festive hall.
