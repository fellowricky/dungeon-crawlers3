A close, tense scene inside a goblin warcamp at night, before a rank of crude war-drums, lit by firelight. No people appear in the scene. The composition centres on three or four large lashed-hide drums of stretched skin over hollowed log-frames, daubed with ugly ochre and dried-blood war-paint, their beater-sticks leaning ready against them. Behind the drums rises the inner stockade of sharpened stakes and lashed timber, and a bonfire off to one side throws hard orange light and long leaping shadows across the trampled earth, catching drifting sparks and thin smoke. Bone fetishes, feathers and knucklebone charms hang from the stakes and the drum-frames. The ground is churned mud strewn with gnawed bones and dropped crude weapons. The drum-skins seem almost to still tremble with sound. Palette of hot orange firelight and sickly ochre against deep smoky black and the harsh red of war-paint. Mood of savage, deliberate menace — a threat being performed.
