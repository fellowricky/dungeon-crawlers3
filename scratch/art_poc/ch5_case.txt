A close, still, ominous interior scene of a single sealed case resting alone on a stone plinth at the centre of a small dry vault chamber. No people and no creatures appear anywhere in the scene. There is no written lettering of any kind anywhere in the image.

The case is the unmistakable focal point, centred and lit: a heavy rectangular strongbox about the size of a large book-chest, built of dark close-grained timber and bound in wide straps of blackened iron with heavy corner brackets and a thick iron carrying handle at each end. Its surfaces are utterly plain — no crest, no maker's mark, no label, no inscription, no letters, no words, no numbers, nothing. It is deliberately, conspicuously anonymous. It sits squarely on the plinth, lid closed, and it reads as far heavier than its size suggests.

Across the seam of the lid is a single blob of deep crimson sealing wax stamped with a simple abstract geometric sigil — interlocking angular shapes only, no letters. Critically, this wax seal is obviously, jarringly newer than everything else: the wax is fresh, glossy, sharp-edged and unblemished, its colour bright, while the case beneath it is old, dull, scuffed and dusty. The contrast between the ancient case and the brand-new seal is the point of the picture.

The plinth is a plain squared block of pale worn stone, standing alone in the middle of a bare flagstone floor. The chamber around it is small, austere and — remarkably — bone dry: tight-fitted ancient masonry walls, a low barrel-vaulted ceiling, dry dust on the floor, not a trickle of water anywhere, despite the black fenwater that must be pressing in on all sides. A heavy iron-bound door stands ajar in the shadows at the back. There is no treasure, no clutter, no other object in the room at all — just the plinth and the case in an empty stone box.

Lighting is a single narrow cold shaft falling from a small grating high in the vault, landing precisely on the case and plinth and throwing a long hard shadow across the flagstones. Everything beyond that pool of light sinks quickly into deep blue-black. Fine dust hangs motionless in the beam.

Palette of cold grey stone, dark timber and black iron, with the one hot crimson accent of the fresh wax. Mood of quiet, deliberate, institutional wrongness — a very ordinary object that somebody has taken great care to tell you not to open.
