#WARM
A warm, crowded scene of a mercenary-charter's betting corner beside a fighting yard at torchlit evening, richly lit. Background spectators appear only as distant, loosely-suggested crowd silhouettes with no clear faces in front. CRITICAL: there is NO writing, NO text, NO letters, NO numbers, NO chalkboard-lettering and NO signage of any kind anywhere in the scene — every board and slate is blank or marked only with abstract non-letter symbols. The focus is a large wooden peg-board mounted on posts, hung with rows of coloured cords, small brass coin-tokens on hooks, wooden tally-pegs and a few blank dark slate tiles — purely abstract markers and colours, no legible characters at all. Beyond, the packed gallery rail and the dark torchlit mouth of the fighting yard glow through drifting haze, plain coloured pennants strung overhead. Warm amber and gold lamplight over honey-brown timber, brass and coin, deep festive shadow behind. Palette of warm gold, amber, banner-red and blue against soft brown dark. Strictly pre-industrial medieval fantasy, nothing modern. Mood of a raucous, money-charged carnival with an ugly secret in the odds.
