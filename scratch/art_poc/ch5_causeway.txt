#LANDSCAPE
A wide, bleak scene of a submerged stone causeway crossing a vast black fen under heavy mist, at cold colourless dawn. No people and no creatures appear anywhere in the scene. A raised drowned road runs from the foreground straight out into the mere, its flagstones just under a skin of still black water so its line is barely readable, edges dissolving into deep peat-bog and reed on either side. Skeletal dead trees, broken marker-posts and a half-sunk boundary stone stand up out of the water at intervals, fading fast into a thick grey wall of fen-mist that closes off all distance to a few yards. The water is glass-still, reflecting the pale featureless sky. Deep atmospheric perspective, everything swallowed by mist. Palette of black water, drowned grey stone, dull reed-brown and colourless pale mist. Mood of a treacherous, near-invisible path — solid footing one step wide with drowning to either side and no warning of what waits in the fog.
