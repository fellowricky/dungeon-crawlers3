#LANDSCAPE
A bleak drowned fenland at grey dusk under heavy mist. A stone causeway runs from the foreground into the distance, its flagstones a hand's depth under still black water so the road reads as a faint pale line beneath the surface. Standing up out of the water along its length are marker-posts — and they are conspicuously fresh: new-cut iron-shod timber posts, driven deep, each burned with a small abstract geometric guild sigil, plainly far newer than the century-drowned road they mark. One older, weathered post leans snapped beside a crisp new replacement.

The fen stretches away flat and black — dead reeds, skeletal drowned trees, standing water, banks of cold mist swallowing the distance. No people, no fire, no smoke. Cold near-monochrome palette of black water, pewter mist, drowned brown reeds, wet grey stone. Mood of a "forgotten" road that someone has been quietly, recently maintaining.
