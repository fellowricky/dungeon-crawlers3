A dramatic boss-reveal of a single huge eyeless subterranean predator rearing up out of the darkness at the bottom of a deep stone shaft, seen from slightly below so it looms over the viewer. No people appear anywhere in the scene.

The creature is a nameless burrowing aberration, and it must look genuinely alien — not a dragon, not a reptile, not a spider, not any familiar animal. Its body is a thick, muscular, segmented worm-like trunk as broad as a horse, rearing up in a coiled S-curve, its lower length still disappearing into a black hole in the stone floor. Its hide is slick rubbery grey-green stone-coloured flesh, banded and ridged, mottled to match wet rock, glistening with slime and streaked with filth.

It has no eyes at all, and no face — the front of its trunk simply opens into a round, radial, lamprey-like maw: a wet circular pit ringed with concentric rows of small hooked grey teeth, gaping directly at the viewer. Around that maw, four long barbed tentacles splay outward and forward, thick as tree limbs, tapering to hooked spines, lined with rough gripping barbs along their inner faces. The tentacles are spread wide in a threat display, some curling, and they frame the maw at the centre of the composition. Ropes of thick saliva string between them.

It is coiled amid the wreckage of its kill. The floor of the shaft is a mound of shattered cattle bones, cracked skulls, horns and torn hide, half-sunk in black mud and standing water. A few pale broken cattle carcasses lie at the edges of frame, indistinct in shadow rather than graphically gory.

The shaft walls rising around it are ancient dressed stone — huge precisely fitted ashlar blocks in regular courses, cracked and shoved outward, wet and slick with slime and dripping water, with dark tunnel mouths gaping behind the creature.

Lighting is a single hard cold shaft of pale grey daylight falling from far above down onto the creature's rearing back and tentacles, catching the slime highlights, with everything else sinking into deep blue-black shadow. Drifting dust motes in the light beam. Cold palette of wet grey stone, sickly grey-green flesh, bone white and black depth, with almost no warm colour. Mood of alien, indifferent, nameless hunger from far underground.
