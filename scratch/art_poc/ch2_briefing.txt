#LANDSCAPE
A wide, bleak establishing landscape of open upland moor under a low overcast sky, at grey overcast midday. No people are visible anywhere in the scene. There are no signs, no signposts, no banners and no written lettering of any kind.

This place is cold, wet and empty. There are no fires, no burning, no embers, no smoke plumes and no columns of smoke anywhere in the image. The only things in the sky are cloud, rain and birds.

The composition is dominated by emptiness and horizontal flatness. Coarse grey-green moor grass, heather, rushes and dark peat bog run unbroken to a very distant flat horizon, with shallow standing pools of brown water reflecting the dull sky. The land is treeless and exposed.

Cutting through the middle of the frame from the foreground into the far distance is an ancient drove-road: a wide churned track of trampled mud and torn turf, gouged deep into the moor like a scar, edged with broken drystone walling. Its surface is a chaos of deep cattle hoofprints and standing water. It runs dead straight toward the horizon and is the strongest line in the composition.

Scattered across the moor on either side are low grassy burial mounds — ancient longbarrows, humped and turf-covered, some with a collapsed stone lintel or a few standing kerb stones exposed at one end. They are the only vertical features in the landscape and they are unmistakably older than the road.

In the middle distance beside the track lies a dark indistinct heap in the grass — something dead and unmoving, too far away to identify.

High above it, a scattering of black carrion birds wheels against the cloud. Each bird is drawn as its own separate small solid silhouette with spread wings, scattered loosely across the sky at different heights. There are no spiral lines, no drawn loops, no circles, no curved trails, no motion streaks and no marks of any kind in the sky apart from the individual bird shapes themselves.

The sky is a heavy unbroken ceiling of low grey and slate-blue cloud pressing down on the land, with a thin pale band of cold light at the horizon. Wind-flattened grass. Cold damp air, distant grey rain veils sweeping the far horizon.

Palette of cold greys, drab olive-green, peat brown, rust-coloured bracken and dead white sky. Mood of huge, exposed, indifferent emptiness — open country with nowhere to hide and something wrong in it.
