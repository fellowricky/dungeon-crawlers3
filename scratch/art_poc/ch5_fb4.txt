A cold, dry underground archive vault of ancient dressed stone, lit by a single hard shaft of pale grey light. Rank on rank of stone shelves recede into shadow, each shelf holding rows of identical iron-bound wooden cases sealed with dark wax — hundreds of them, filed like records, each with a small pale tag tied to its handle (the tags rendered as blank or illegible, no readable letters). In the foreground, one shelf has a single empty gap, dusted and waiting, with a small blank tag already tied to the empty space where a case will go.

The framing centres on that one waiting empty slot among the full shelves. Cold palette of grey dressed stone, dark iron-bound cases, black shadow, dust hanging in the light shaft. No people. Mood of an archive of sealed lives with one slot reserved and waiting — a filing system for deaths not yet come.
