#LANDSCAPE
A wide, bleak establishing scene of a muddy drove-road churned across open moorland, at flat grey overcast dawn. No people and no creatures appear anywhere in the scene. The broad track runs from the foreground away into misty distance, its surface a hundred paces of deep trampled mud — a chaos of overlapping cattle hoofprints, and running level among them a second, narrower set of prints, spaced too far and too even, keeping pace with the herd rather than following it. Broken fence-rails and a dropped, mud-trodden coil of rope lie at the verge. To either side the moor rolls away in tussock, heather and grey rock under a vast heavy pewter sky, a far line of dark hills lost in rain-mist. Deep atmospheric perspective. Muted palette of brown churned mud, bleached grass, dull heather-purple and cold grey sky. Mood of a chase read backward from the ground — something ran with the cattle, not after them.
