#LANDSCAPE
A grim moorland slope under heavy grey overcast. Below a low rocky bluff, a whole flock of dead sheep lies heaped and scattered across the grass — dozens of pale woolly bodies piled where they fell at the foot of the drop, tangled together, legs and necks at wrong angles. They are clearly killed by the fall, unmarked by any predator, none of them eaten, just left to lie.

Above them the lip of the bluff is torn to churned mud by countless small hooves, all pointing off the edge. The moor stretches away bleak and empty beyond — coarse grass, heather, standing water, a flat misty horizon under dead grey sky. No people, no predator, no fire, no smoke. Muted cold palette of dirty wool-white, drab moor green, peat brown, wet grey rock and pale dead sky. Mood of a senseless mass death — animals driven off a height in the dark by something that never stopped to feed.
