A dramatic boss-reveal of a single enormous caged owlbear inside a torchlit exhibition arena, seen from ringside and slightly below so the beast towers over the viewer. No human faces are visible anywhere — any spectators are distant backlit silhouettes only.

The owlbear fills the centre of the frame, hunched furiously against the bars of a heavy iron-barred cage on a low wooden cart. It is a true owlbear: the massive muscular body, hunched shoulders and heavy clawed forelimbs of a great bear, thickly furred in shaggy red-brown, transitioning at the neck into the head of a giant owl — a broad flat feathered facial disc, a huge hooked bone-yellow beak opened in a scream, and a ruff of bristling barred feathers standing erect around the face. Its two enormous round eyes are brilliant molten orange-yellow with hard black pupils, glaring straight through the bars at the viewer with pure furious hatred. Feathers and fur blend along its shoulders and back. Its claws are splayed and gouging the cage floor.

It is visibly, actively enraged and cramped: one huge shoulder is jammed against the bars, several iron bars are visibly bent outward where it has been working at them, the cage is far too small for it, and splintered wood and scattered straw litter the cart bed. Its beak is open mid-shriek, tongue visible, a fleck of spit. It is unhurt and unbloodied — this animal has not been wounded, only caged and goaded.

Immediately in front of it in the foreground, slightly out of focus, run the thick woven ropes of a fighting ring, strung between heavy wooden posts, cutting across the bottom of the frame — the viewer is standing inside the ring, on the sand, looking at what is about to be released.

Behind and around the cage, the arena is a warm-lit wooden exhibition hall: a raked timber gallery packed with a crowd rendered only as small dark backlit silhouettes and warm indistinct shapes with no discernible faces, plus rows of burning torches and hanging lanterns along the walls, and bright heraldic bunting of plain solid colours bearing simple abstract geometric shapes and no letters or writing.

Lighting is hot and theatrical: strong warm torchlight raking across the beast's feathers and fur from the left, hard rim-light along its shoulders, glinting off the iron bars and its wet beak and eyes, with the crowd and gallery falling into warm brown-black shadow behind. Drifting dust and floating feather-down in the torch beams. Palette of rich red-brown fur, tawny barred feathers, hot orange torchlight, black iron and warm timber. Mood of penned-up violence about to be let loose for entertainment.
