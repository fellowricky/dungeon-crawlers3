#WARM
A warm, torchlit betting stand at the edge of a wooden fighting-arena, at night. The focus is a large chalk odds-board covered in illegible chalked odds and tallies (loose chalk marks, no readable letters or numbers), and below it a table where a bookmaker's rake pulls in stacks and scattered piles of coins. Beyond the stand, the raked timber gallery of a packed arena is suggested by warm backlit crowd silhouettes and rows of torches — no discernible faces, just dark shapes and firelight.

The whole scene glows warm and loud. Hot torchlight, honey timber, glinting coin. Bright heraldic bunting in solid colours with simple abstract shapes, no letters. Palette of orange torch-glow, warm wood, gold coin against dark crowd-shadow. Mood of a rigged, cheerful mill where the house rakes every wager and never loses.
